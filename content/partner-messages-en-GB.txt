RULES / IMPLEMENTATION
========================================================================================
• Phase does not determine someone's mood, energy, confidence, desire or consent; use
  opt-in, non-diagnostic language.
• No check-in shared = no claims about how she feels.
• Plain ("straight") tips during period days, TTC, a late period, or a rough day,
  whatever his tone. Period humour (T05–T12, T73) shows only if she turns on
  "Humour during my period too" on her Partner tab; rough days stay plain even then.
• Phase info, predicted dates and symptoms appear only if she shares them.
• G02 only when she logged the start. G31/G32 are predictions and never say it started.
• Predicted ovulation is never presented as contraception.
• His replies are suggestions: nothing is sent until he taps one, and R06/R07 let him
  say "later" or "not today".
• Each person picks their own language, before signing in and later in Settings.
• Codes never change meaning and are never reused. Retired: A15, A29.
• "Peak" is still the internal name; everyone sees "Mid-cycle".
• U lines follow "Tomorrow:" or "In about 3 days:".
