KEY DECISIONS
• Quiet days: no forced insight. Keep an optional plain cycle-day heading.
• Seven-day repeat: too frequent as a default; aim 14–30 days for common symptoms, unless new information justifies it.
• High sex drive: no automatic card or assumption. L06 is for positive feelings the user actually logged.
• C07 merged: cannot infer night sweats from hot flushes + short sleep.
• L20 and C02 BLOCKED pending individual clinician-reviewed urgent-care logic and privacy review.
• M03 becomes an unobtrusive UI prompt, not an “insight” and never silently completes a period.
• Pattern (P02) is a descriptive log statistic, NOT medically validated inference.
• Personal opening lines are optional and must never crowd out clear safety advice.
• Correct links for new NHS menopause and headache pages are in structured JSON.

DISPLAY/RUNTIME LOGIC
• For ordinary content show at most one contextual card per day; no forced filler on quiet days. Safety alert uses its own clinician-reviewed route and may pre-empt ordinary content.
• User must explicitly log the symptom that triggers a symptom card; missing data is unknown, not a negative finding.
• The order is: clinician-approved urgent red-flag routing based on explicit responses > relevant time-sensitive optional guidance > same-day matching symptom > meaningful personal-pattern insight > opted-in phase/cycle education.
• Combination cards require both symptoms explicitly logged in the relevant same-day event; never infer causation or severity from their coexistence.
• Opening lines optional; show only if data quality supports them. Never assert medical interpretations, outcomes or “first ever” beyond MoonCycle records.
• Allow dismiss, “not relevant”, snooze and per-topic opt-outs; avoid repeating a card merely because it has a different wording.
• Use concept-level cooldowns: ordinary symptoms 14–30 days (or a material change); phase education once per cycle, general education 30–90 days, safety only when supported by clinically reviewed red flags.
• If no relevant card meets selection rules, show no card; the dashboard can still show a neutral cycle-day label.
• Distinguish predicted dates from confirmed period starts; require observation completeness and avoid clinical conclusions from cycle estimates.
• Fertility information only when opted in, and never recommend calendar dates for avoiding pregnancy; apply pregnancy/contraception/postpartum/breastfeeding/HRT and other relevant model exclusions with clinician input.
• One-tap partner unpairing and sharing preferences remain independent of Her card selection. Her sensitive card contents must not be shared or shown in lock-screen push notifications without separate opt-in.
• For emergency-contraception help, include contraceptive failure even if hormonal contraception is used; expose accessible on-demand advice without disclosing sex history.
• For UK use correct NHS contact routing; international versions need separately approved service advice rather than just translated UK 111/999 text.
• Maintain always-accessible urgent mental-health help, but do not automatically push crisis messaging simply because sad/anxious mood was logged.
• Clinician must sign off on red-flag symptom questions, severity thresholds, displayed emergency text and operational triage before production.
