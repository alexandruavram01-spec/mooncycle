HOW A CARD IS CHOSEN (lib/domain/her_insights.dart)
========================================================================================
Order, most important first:
1. Safety — only on something she reported.
   • I21: bleeding logged on a day that is not part of a logged period, after
     her first logged period. Never from predicted dates.
2. What she logged — today, or yesterday if today has nothing yet.
   Two things logged the same day, before either on its own:
   • Cramps + heavy flow → C01   • Headache + feeling sick → C03
   • Two of bloating, cravings, tender breasts, before her period → C04
   • Sad/anxious/irritable + under 6 hours' sleep → C05
   • Tiredness or energy 1–2 + under 6 hours' sleep → C06
   • Low desire + sad/anxious, only with "Cards about sex and desire" on → C08
   One thing (a card with a personal opening line first):
   • Cramps: near her period → I01, otherwise → L01   • Heavy flow → I02
   • Tiredness → I19 (I05 if heavy flow this cycle); energy 1–2 → I19
   • Headache: near her period → L05, otherwise → I16
   • Backache: near her period → L02, otherwise → L03
   • Ovulation pain → I10   • Bloating → I17   • Breast tenderness → I18
   • Sad/anxious/irritable/sensitive → I20; sad or anxious on 4+ days over
     2+ weeks → L07
   • Egg-white discharge: trying to conceive → I30; fertility on → L18;
     otherwise → L17   • Dry/sticky/creamy/watery → L17
   • Joint pain L04 · Spots L09 · Feeling sick L10 · Dizziness L11 (not with a
     heavy flow: that is C02, on hold) · Hot flush L12 · Cravings L13 ·
     Constipation L14 · Diarrhoea L15 · Appetite L16 · Low desire L19 ·
     Under 6 hours' sleep on 2 of the last 3 nights L21
   • Happy/calm/confident → L06, only when nothing else applies
   • No card: protected or unprotected sex (L20 blocked), high drive
   "Near her period" = during it, or up to 2 days before the estimate.
3. Her own history.
   • Period 3+ days late, prediction confidence medium or high → I22
     (I23 instead if trying to conceive)
   • 8+ days of bleeding in a row → I24
   • 2 of the last 3 cycles outside 21–35 days, or the last 2 far from her
     usual → I25
   • Tiredness with heavy flow in 2 or more cycles → I05
   • Mood changes in the last 7 days of 3+ recorded cycles, and now in that
     part of her cycle → L08
   • H01–H03 below (drafts)
4. Moments in her cycle (once a cycle), then general information.
   • Period day 1–2 → M01   • Day 3+, bleeding logged today, not heavy → M02
   • Estimated fertile window starts (fertility on) → M04
   • 1–2 days before the estimated period → M05
   • Period: I01, I02, I03   • After the period: I06, I07, I11*
   • Mid-cycle: I09*, I10*, I11*
   • Before the period: I12, I13 (3–7 days before the estimate), I14 (2+ cycles)
   • Estimated day to 2 days after: NEW01   • NEW02 (hormonal contraception)
   • I08 only if she turned on screening reminders in Settings
   • Trying to conceive: I26, I27, I29, I28 (trying over 12 months)
   * only with the fertility journey or fertile days on

Opening lines (P01–P12): the first that fits, only from entries she made.
• About what she logged: P02 (same part of the cycle in 2+ of 3+ recorded
  cycles), P01 (days in a row), P03 (same point last cycle), P04 (first time).
  None for sleep numbers or sex.
• Otherwise by card: period day and history P05, estimate P06, past the
  estimate P07, start versus estimate P08 (not when trying to conceive),
  cycle lengths P09, sleep P10, energy P11, fertile window P12.
• The same line about the same thing rests 21 days. None on safety cards.

Rules:
• One card a day; a second only when the first is a safety card.
• Today's card stays the same all day unless something more important comes
  up, or what it answered is no longer logged (then the next card shows).
• Within a tier: combinations, then cards with a personal line, then the
  rest; never shown first, then shown longest ago. Rest periods from the
  packs; a second wording shares its card's rest period.
• Nothing new to say: no card.
• × hides a card for today. "Not relevant to me" hides it until she brings
  hidden cards back in Settings.
• Hormonal contraception, pregnancy/breastfeeding, hysterectomy or HRT (from her
  quiz): no cycle-range, late-period, ovulation or "estimates" cards.
• Only the cycle and fertility journeys get cards.
• Crisis help is on the "Get help now" page (Today's top bar, Settings, and
  inside any card with urgent advice), never pushed because of a mood.
• Everything runs on her phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

NEW — CARDS FROM HER OWN LOG (drafts for your review)
========================================================================================
No health claims: they repeat facts from her own log. Source line on the card:
"Based on what you logged in MoonCycle".

[ ] H01  A pattern in your log
         Shown when: the same pain, mood or body symptom was logged in the same
         part of the cycle in at least 60% of her last 3+ completed cycles, and
         she is in that part of her cycle today. Never fluid or sex entries.
         "You've logged {headaches} {in the days before your period} in {3} of
         your last {4} cycles. Knowing your pattern can help you plan ahead, and
         your notes can be useful if you ever speak to a GP."
         Cooldown: 21 days

[ ] H02  Your cycles lately
         Shown when: her last 3 cycles were all 21–35 days and within 4 days of
         each other. Not with hormonal contraception.
         "Your last 3 cycles were {27, 29 and 28} days long. Steady cycles make
         predictions more useful, though they are still estimates."
         Cooldown: 60 days

[ ] H03  Your energy across your cycle
         Shown when: 2+ completed cycles, at least 3 energy entries in two parts
         of her cycle, and a difference of 1 point or more on the 1–5 scale.
         "In what you've logged, your energy has tended to be highest {in the
         days after your period} and lowest {during your period}. Everyone's
         pattern is different."
         Cooldown: 45 days

URGENT HELP: WHERE TO TURN, NOT DETAILED ADVICE (decided 21 October 2026)
========================================================================================
Cards no longer show detailed warning signs (the "Get help now" text of each
card is kept in the code but not shown). Any card that had it, the safety
card, and every card about something she logged (except L19 and C08, about
desire) shows one plain line instead, always visible:
  "Not feeling well? Call 999 in an emergency, or 111 for urgent advice (in
  Northern Ireland, your GP)."   [Get help now]

"GET HELP NOW" PAGE
Intro: If you don't feel well or you're worried, these services can help,
wherever you are in the UK. MoonCycle can't give medical advice or check on you.
• 999: emergencies — Call 999, or go to A&E, in an emergency or if someone's
  life may be at risk. This works everywhere in the UK.   [Call 999]
• 111: urgent advice — When it isn't an emergency but you need advice soon, call
  111 in England, Scotland or Wales. In England you can also use NHS 111
  online, and choose the mental health option if that's what you need.
  [Call 111] [111.nhs.uk (England)]
• In Northern Ireland — For urgent advice, call your GP. When your surgery is
  closed, call the GP out-of-hours service for your area; the numbers are on
  nidirect.   [nidirect: GP out of hours]
• Samaritans: someone to talk to — Call 116 123 to talk to someone, about
  anything, at any time.   [Call 116 123]
• Your GP — For anything that is worrying you but is not urgent, book an
  appointment with your GP.
Footer: Outside the UK, call your local emergency number.
Sources: S17 (urgent mental health help), S23 (ectopic pregnancy), S9 (toxic
shock syndrome).
