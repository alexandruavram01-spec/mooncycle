========================================================================================
HOW A CARD IS CHOSEN (lib/domain/her_insights.dart)
========================================================================================
Order, most important first:
1. Safety — only on something she reported.
   • I21: bleeding logged on a day that is not part of a logged period, after
     her first logged period. Never from predicted dates.
2. What she logged — today, or yesterday if today has nothing yet.
   • Cramps on or near a period → I01   • Heavy flow → I02
   • Tiredness → I19, or I05 if heavy flow this cycle   • Ovulation pain → I10
   • Headache → I16   • Bloating → I17   • Breast tenderness → I18
   • Sad / Anxious / Irritable / Sensitive → I20
   • Egg-white discharge, trying to conceive → I30
3. Her own history.
   • Period 3+ days late, prediction confidence medium or high → I22
     (I23 instead if trying to conceive)
   • 8+ days of bleeding in a row → I24
   • 2 of the last 3 cycles outside 21–35 days, or the last 2 far from her
     usual → I25
   • Tiredness with heavy flow in 2 or more cycles → I05
   • H01–H03 below (drafts)
4. Cycle education.
   • Period: I01, I02, I03   • After the period: I06, I07, I11*
   • Mid-cycle: I09*, I10*, I11*   • Before the period: I12, I13, I14 (2+ cycles)
   • Any day: NEW01 (after 1st cycle), NEW02 (hormonal contraception),
     I08 (only if she turned on screening reminders in Settings)
   • Trying to conceive: I26, I27, I29, I28 (trying over 12 months)
   * only with the fertility journey or fertile days on

Rules:
• One card a day; a second only when the first is a safety card.
• Today's card stays the same all day unless something more important comes up.
• Within a tier: never shown first, then shown longest ago. Cooldowns from the pack.
• × hides a card for today. "Not relevant to me" hides it until she brings
  hidden cards back in Settings.
• Hormonal contraception, pregnancy/breastfeeding, hysterectomy or HRT (from her
  quiz): no cycle-range, late-period, ovulation or "estimates" cards.
• Only the cycle and fertility journeys get cards.
• Crisis help is on the "Get help now" page (Today's top bar, Settings, and
  inside any card with urgent advice), never pushed because of a mood.
• Everything runs on her phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

NEW — CARDS FROM HER OWN LOG (drafts for your review)
========================================================================================
No health claims: they repeat facts from her own log. Source line on the card:
"Based on what you logged in MoonCycle".

[ ] H01  A pattern in your log
         Shown when: the same pain, mood or body symptom was logged in the same
         part of the cycle in at least 60% of her last 3+ completed cycles, and
         she is in that part of her cycle today. Never fluid or sex entries.
         "You've logged {headaches} {in the days before your period} in {3} of
         your last {4} cycles. Knowing your pattern can help you plan ahead, and
         your notes can be useful if you ever speak to a GP."
         Cooldown: 21 days

[ ] H02  Your cycles lately
         Shown when: her last 3 cycles were all 21–35 days and within 4 days of
         each other. Not with hormonal contraception.
         "Your last 3 cycles were {27, 29 and 28} days long. Steady cycles make
         predictions more useful, though they are still estimates."
         Cooldown: 60 days

[ ] H03  Your energy across your cycle
         Shown when: 2+ completed cycles, at least 3 energy entries in two parts
         of her cycle, and a difference of 1 point or more on the 1–5 scale.
         "In what you've logged, your energy has tended to be highest {in the
         days after your period} and lowest {during your period}. Everyone's
         pattern is different."
         Cooldown: 45 days

Intro: If you need help now, these are the services in the UK. MoonCycle cannot
give medical advice or check on you.

• NHS 111: urgent advice — For urgent medical advice when it is not an
  emergency, call 111 or use NHS 111 online. For your mental health, choose the
  mental health option.   [Call 111] [111.nhs.uk]
• Samaritans: someone to talk to — Call 116 123 to talk to someone, about
  anything, at any time.   [Call 116 123]
• Your GP — For anything that is worrying you but is not urgent, book an
  appointment with your GP.
Footer: Outside the UK, call your local emergency number.

shock syndrome).
