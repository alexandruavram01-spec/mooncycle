Delete your MoonCycle account and data

MoonCycle by IONCODEX LTD

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open MoonCycle and go to Settings. On the partner's screen, open the menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Delete my account and data (or Account, then Delete account).
  3. Confirm with your phone's fingerprint, face or PIN (or, if your phone has no screen lock, your password, or your Google account if you signed up with Google).

Your account is deleted from our server straight away, together with everything linked to it, and MoonCycle erases what it stored on that device. Copies in your phone's own backup (iCloud or your Google account) are not affected: delete them in your phone's or account's settings if you want them gone.

Without the app

Email mooncycle_contact@ioncodex.org from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my MoonCycle account". We will delete your account within one month and confirm by email. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, password hash or Google account ID, and sign-in sessions.
  • Your consent records and tip ratings.
  • Partner sharing: the shared summary, pairing codes, messages, and your connection to a partner.

What is not on our server

What you log (periods, symptoms, notes) stays on your phone and never reaches us. Deleting your account in the app erases it from that device; uninstalling the app does too. Signing out does not. If you only email us, delete the app from your devices as well. Neither reaches your phone's own backup, which you manage yourself.

What we keep

Server logs, which may contain your IP address, are deleted automatically within 14 days. AI-written notes you reported are kept for up to 90 days without your name or account, so they cannot be traced back to you or deleted with it. We keep nothing else.

What your partner has already seen cannot be taken back, but from the moment you delete your account their app shows nothing about you.
