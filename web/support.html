Common questions

Where is my data?

What you log stays on your phone, not on our servers. Your phone's own backup (iCloud, or your Google account on Android) can keep a copy. Without it, if you uninstall MoonCycle or lose your phone, your logs cannot be recovered.

I forgot my password

Password reset is not available yet. If you signed up with Google or Apple, sign in with the same one. Otherwise, email us.

How do I connect my partner?

In MoonCycle, open the Partner tab, turn sharing on and give your partner the code. They enter it in their own MoonCycle. You choose what they see, and you can disconnect at any time.

How do I delete my account?

Settings, then Delete my account and data. See how to delete your account for details.
