Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-26 · Last updated 26 October 2026

The short version
  • What you log (periods, symptoms, mood, notes) stays on your phone. We never receive it.
  • Our server holds your email, a scrambled form of your password (or, if you continue with Google or Apple, an ID for that account), and, only if you turn on partner sharing, the summary you choose to share.
  • Only if you turn on AI-written notes, a short summary of your day passes through our server to Anthropic, which writes the note. We don't store the summary or the note, unless you report a note: then we keep that note and its card, without your name or account, for up to 90 days to look into it and improve the notes. Anthropic deletes what we send within 30 days and doesn't use it to train AI.
  • No advertising, no selling of data, no tracking or analytics tools.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time, in the app. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or your Google account) is separate: you manage it, and we cannot reach it.

What stays on your phone

Everything you log (period dates and flow, symptoms, mood, energy, sleep, discharge, sex, notes), your quiz answers, your reminders and your insight cards are stored only on your device, and in your phone’s own backup if you use one (iCloud, or your Google account on Android), which Apple or Google holds for you under your own account. We do not receive it and cannot see it (except the short daily summary described above, if you turn on AI-written notes). MoonCycle processes it on your phone to show predictions, insights and reminders, with your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)), which you give when you create your account.

Deleting your account in the app, or uninstalling the app, erases what MoonCycle stored on that phone. Signing out does not: your logs stay on the phone. Copies already in your phone's backup are not affected by either. They stay until that backup is replaced or you delete it in your phone's or account's settings, and they may be restored if you reinstall MoonCycle from that backup. We cannot see, change or delete your backups.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data, or share it for advertising.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We send nothing to AI services unless you turn on AI-written notes, and then only the short daily summary described above.
  • We make no decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. Predictions and insight cards are worked out automatically on your phone, and AI-written notes are written from those cards, for information only.

4. Who else is involved

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) hosts our server in its data centre in Germany, under a data processing agreement. The UK recognises the EU as giving adequate protection to personal data.
  • Anthropic PBC (United States) writes AI-written notes for us, only if you turn them on, as our processor under its data processing terms, which include safeguards for transfers from the UK. It deletes what we send within 30 days (longer, up to 2 years, only if its safety systems flag misuse) and does not use it to train its AI models.
  • Account emails: MoonCycle does not send any yet. Before we start (for example for password resets), we will name the email provider here and say what it receives.
  • Google Fonts: the app currently downloads one typeface from Google's servers, which means your device's IP address is visible to Google when it does. We plan to include the typeface in the app instead.
  • Google confirms who you are if you choose Continue with Google, and tells us only your email address and an ID for your Google account. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
  • Apple confirms who you are if you choose Sign in with Apple on an iPhone, and tells us only an email address (yours, or a private relay address) and an ID for your Apple account. When you delete your MoonCycle account, we ask Apple to remove the link. Apple's own privacy policy applies to your Apple account.
  • Apple and Google provide the app stores. When you download MoonCycle, their own privacy policies apply to that.

If your partner connects with you, they see only the summary and messages you choose to share. They are responsible for treating it with respect. What they have already seen cannot be taken back: turning sharing off or disconnecting stops anything new, removes the shared summary from our server, and their app then shows nothing about you.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account, consent records and ratings: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: until you sign out or delete your account, or after 90 days without use, when you are asked to sign in again.
  • Pairing codes: deleted when used, or 24 hours after they are created.
  • The shared summary: replaced each time it updates, and deleted straight away when you turn sharing off or delete your account.
  • One-tap messages and the replies to them: deleted 24 hours after they are sent, or sooner if replaced by a new one or sharing ends.
  • AI-written notes: our server passes the summary to Anthropic and the note back to your phone, and keeps neither. Your phone keeps the note for that day. If you report a note, we keep that note and the card it came from, without your name or account, for up to 90 days, to look into it and improve the notes. Because they are not linked to your account, we cannot find or delete them for you before then.
  • Server logs: up to 14 days.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to how we use it, receive it in a portable form, and withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect what we did before you withdrew it).

  • Delete everything: in the app, go to Settings, then "Delete my account and data". You can also ask by email: see how to delete your account.
  • Take a copy: in the app, go to Settings, then "Export my data". It saves your logs and quiz answers as two files (a spreadsheet and a full copy) wherever you choose. Because your logs are only on your phone, this is also the way to keep them if you change or lose your phone.
  • Stop sharing: turn partner sharing off in the app. The shared summary is removed from our server immediately.
  • Anything else: email mooncycle_contact@ioncodex.org. We will reply within one month.

Data that is only on your phone is under your control: we cannot see, export or delete it for you, but you can export it yourself (above). Deleting your account in the app, or uninstalling the app, erases it from that phone, but not from your phone's own backup (see "What stays on your phone" above).

7. Age

MoonCycle is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly hold data about anyone younger. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as scrypt hashes. Access to our server is restricted. Most of your information never leaves your phone in the first place.

9. Cookies and storage

MoonCycle does not use cookies. The app and the website store your settings and your sign-in on your own device, which is strictly necessary for them to work.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the app will ask you to read and agree to the new version before you continue.
